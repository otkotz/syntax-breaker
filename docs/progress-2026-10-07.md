## Delivered

- Returning projectiles cache the return target, sample trails every 12 pixels instead of every
  physics tick, cap trail history at 12 positions, avoid redundant redraws for trail-less
  projectiles, and cannot decrement the global active count twice.
- Shield Support creates a proximity challenge: ordinary linked enemies reject damage while the
  player is outside every protecting 150px aura. Entering any relevant aura restores full damage.
  Bosses and support-role enemies are excluded.
- The first normal reward slot is explicitly drawn from compatible skills, supports, passives, or
  mutations. The remaining slots preserve direction/flexible drafting and exhausted catalogs fall
  back to gold without blocking the route.
- Persistent cosmetic points award 1 for normal/elite combat, 2 once per miniboss, and 5 for a
  regional boss. Purchases save immediately; old profiles migrate with zero points. `Ember Script`
  costs 5 and `Void Script` costs 10; neither changes mechanics.
- The rare `Laser` support converts projectile delivery into instantaneous rays, preserves the
  shared crit/on-hit/DoT/proc/kill/telemetry path, supports projectile spread plus mine/totem
  delivery, and conflicts with chain, pierce, returning, ricochet, and split in both link orders.
  It includes `assets/icons/supports/laser-v1.png`.
